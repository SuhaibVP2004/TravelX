Our Story

TravelX began in 2014 with two friends, a laptop and a backpacking trip through Southeast Asia that went wonderfully wrong and right at the same time.

Today our team of travel designers plans trips across 60+ countries, working directly with local guides, hotels and drivers so every trip feels personal.
